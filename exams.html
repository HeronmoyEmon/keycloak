Milestone exams

Five self-check exams, one per content-mastery gate (CLAUDE.md §8), each with a cumulative graded quiz pool that crosses chapters rather than re-asking any single chapter's own quiz, plus a short essay section framed as "defend your answer to an interviewer." None of these block progress — scores aren't gated or reported anywhere outside your own browser's localStorage, same as every chapter quiz. They exist so you can find out now whether a Part actually stuck, instead of finding out in a real interview.

Key idea

Take an exam once you've finished the Part(s) it covers and the course has moved past the matching gate — going in cold on content you haven't read yet will just show you what's ahead, not what you've retained.

Exam: Foundations

Chapters 0-9 (Part 0 + Part I) — matches GATE-2. The vendor-agnostic IAM concepts every later chapter assumes: identity lifecycle, AAA, directories, MFA, authorization models, SSO/federation, tokens/sessions, and the threat landscape.

Take the exam

Exam: Protocols

Chapters 10-18 (Part II + Part III) — matches GATE-3. Governance/compliance fundamentals plus real protocol mechanics: OAuth grant types, PKCE, OIDC vs. SAML, JWT/JWS/JWE, and the BCP security practices. The last checkpoint before Keycloak itself enters the course.

Take the exam

Exam: Keycloak Core

Chapters 19-26 (Part IV) — matches GATE-4. The first Keycloak-specific exam: realms, clients, client scopes, protocol mappers, authentication flows, and theming, grounded in the real northwind-apps realm these chapters' own labs actually built.

Take the exam

Exam: Integration

Chapters 27-38 (Part V + Part VI + Part VII) — matches GATE-5. The widest-scope exam: admin API/CLI automation, events/auditing, sessions, password/MFA policy, identity brokering, LDAP federation, a custom User Storage SPI, securing a real backend and frontend, service-to-service auth, UMA 2.0 authorization, and ABAC/ReBAC vs. a dedicated policy engine.

Take the exam

Exam: Final (comprehensive)

Chapters 39-48 plus the capstone, and a cumulative review across the entire course — matches the Final QA gate. The biggest exam: extending Keycloak via custom SPIs, production/scale/hardening, the three case studies, synthesis questions bridging Part I-III concepts to their Keycloak implementation, and several questions grounded directly in the real, running capstone build.
