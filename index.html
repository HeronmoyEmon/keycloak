IAM & Keycloak Mastery

A from-zero course for becoming an IAM specialist, not a Keycloak button-clicker. Every concept chapter shows how Keycloak actually implements the idea, and every Keycloak chapter opens by naming which general IAM concept it's an instance of — so you come away able to explain why identity systems are built the way they are, with a real local Keycloak instance, graded quizzes, and a defensible capstone to show for it.

Key idea

Three running organizations anchor every worked example from chapter 2 onward, instead of one-off made-up companies: Northwind Apps (a B2B SaaS company needing multi-tenant CIAM and enterprise SSO for its customers), Riverside Health (a hospital network balancing HIPAA compliance with a patient portal and clinician workforce IAM), and Meridian Trust (a retail bank juggling PCI-DSS, step-up auth, and fraud-aware authentication). When you see those names later in a chapter, you'll already know which kind of identity problem is in play.
